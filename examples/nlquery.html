Queries in English

  • By id
  • Get post id 5 A single node, addressed by a field called id.
  • Get posts id 4, 5, 8, 10 A list of ids, parsed into $in.
  • Get posts id between 4, 10 A range, parsed into $between.
  • Ordering and limits
  • Get posts order desc by id limit 5 Sorting and a ceiling, both from the sentence.
  • Get posts order desc by views limit 5 The five most-read posts of the set.
  • Text
  • …whose title starts with H Starts with, ordered by the same field.
  • …whose body contains love Contains, applied to one named field.
  • Full text search "fiction" Across every field of every node, not one in particular.
  • Fields and membership
  • …whose tags includes history Reaches inside an array field.
  • …whose views between 1000 and 2500 A numeric range over real values.
