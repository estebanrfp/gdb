Where everyone is looking

Calibrate once against nine points and your gaze becomes a point every other window can see, in your own colour. Wink and that point rings — left eye, right eye or both — in every window at once. Open this page again to watch, or calibrate there too and compare.

The camera is used only to track: it is never shown, and no frame leaves this browser — what travels is a point. About 4 MB of face model downloads the first time, plus the vision runtime. Needs https away from localhost. Hold your head still while the nine dots run, and press C to calibrate again whenever you move.
