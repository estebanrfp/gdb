Outliner

A Workflowy-style outline where the tree is the graph itself: every bullet is a node and its children are its edges, so moving a branch is one unlink and one link — nothing is re-serialized, however deep the branch. Enter splits a bullet, Backspace at its start merges it into the one above, Tab/Shift+Tab move it across the tree, Alt+↑↓ reorder it, Ctrl/⌘+↑↓ fold it.

On the right, the same outline drawn as the graph it is stored as: a dot per node, a line per edge, laid out by forces — drag a dot and the rest gives way. Click a dot, here or there, to zoom in: the outline re-roots on that bullet and the search runs $edge from it, through every descendant at any depth, in one query. Open a second window and move a whole branch: it swings to its new parent on the drawing, and the other side received two edge changes, not a document.
