    Guided scenario — authority outlives the signer's connection

    A role assignment is signed data, not a live permission check. Once the superadmin signs it, any peer can carry it — so it can reach someone the signer never meets. Alice's window stays open throughout: she is the relay.

    1. As Superadmin, promote Alice to manager. Her role updates live.
    2. Close the superadmin's browser. The peer count drops — the signer is offline.
    3. Open Bob. Only Alice is online, and Bob is a guest.
    4. Close Bob's browser.
    5. Reopen Superadmin and promote Bob by address, with Bob offline. Alice receives and stores the signed grant.
    6. Close the superadmin again. Only Alice holds Bob's promotion now.
    7. Reopen Bob. Alice — not the superadmin — relays the grant, and Bob's role rises, verified against the superadmin's signature.

    Pick an identity

    Run this in three separate browsers or profiles — not tabs. Same-origin tabs sync locally through BroadcastChannel and skip the P2P mesh, so the propagation would not be real. Superadmin grants roles; Alice and Bob start as zero-trust guests.
