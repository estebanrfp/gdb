    Pick an identity

    Open this in two or three windows. Sign in as Superadmin in one: its window runs the engine that promotes guests. Sign in as Alice and Bob in the others — they arrive as zero-trust guests, are promoted to user after five seconds, and can then create notes and grant each other read, write or delete on them, one note at a time.

    Throwaway identities with their phrases in the source, shared with the other security examples. Never do this in a real application.
